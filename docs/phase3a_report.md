## 1. Guard

`load_explore_runs(day, cell=None, symbols=None)` returns `{symbol: runs}` for exploration stocks only. It
raises `ValueError` before opening any file for:
- a day outside the universe table's `# study_days` header;
- an unknown cell;
- any requested symbol that is not an exploration stock (confirmation, ineligible, unknown).

A runs file is one compressed array holding every stock of the day, so its bytes must be inflated. It is
streamed in chunks of 2^20 records. Each chunk keeps only rows whose locate maps, through that day's
symbol table, to a requested exploration symbol, so no confirmation row outlives its chunk buffer. A second
check raises if any unrequested locate reaches the output. All Phase 3 code reads real data only through
this function (`calib3.collect` is its only caller).

Columns: N per stock-day quantiles. N_min is the fewest runs of a stock over its 8 days (minimum and median
over the cell's stocks; the per-stock values are in `calibration_stocks.tsv`). mu and sigma_l are the
log-normal MLE. rho_logq is the ACF of log q, demeaned per stock-day and pooled. C(l) is the uncentred sign
ACF pooled within cell and within stock-day. "gamma fit" is the OLS of log C on log l over the fit lags,
with a cluster-bootstrap (stock-day) 95 % interval. "gamma LW" is local Whittle on stock-days with
N >= 500, inverse-variance pooled (n stock-days, Cochran Q / df).

The second table gives the sign model behind the generators: Gaussian ACF w1 rho_H(l) + w2 phi^l, eps =
sign(g), fitted to sin(pi C / 2) on lags 1..L_max. It also gives the size copula (log q ACF w rho_H(l)).

**Measurement (calibration).**
- These are thin stocks. The median runs per stock-day is 18 to 1557 by cell, and 10 % of stock-days in
  N-T1S1 have 3 runs or fewer.
- C(l) has two parts. A fast part falls from 0.36 to 0.59 at lag 1 to 0.01 to 0.09 at lag 10. Beyond
  that a slow, nearly flat tail sits at 0.015 to 0.04 out to lags of 100 to 500.
- The power-law exponent on that tail is near 0 in most cells (-0.08 to 0.35). Local Whittle within
  stock-days gives 0.70 to 0.94. The two methods disagree by 0.5 to 0.9.
- The pooled uncentred C(l) contains the variance of each stock-day's mean sign, which local Whittle
  (frequencies j >= 1 only) never sees. Checked on signs alone: at lag 100 the pair-weighted mean of
  m_sd^2 is 0.008 to 0.032, against an uncentred C(100) of 0.012 to 0.038. With each stock-day demeaned,
  C(100) drops to 0.002 to 0.021 (this estimator is itself biased down under memory). So most of the
  "tail" is heterogeneity of daily imbalance across stock-days, not memory within a day. Cochran's Q / df
  of 2.1 to 6.6 says the same.
- In 8 of 18 cells the fGn component sits on the grid edge H = 0.995 with weight 0.02 to 0.05. The
  generators reproduce this as a near-constant sign bias per day.
- The log q ACF is weak (0.07 to 0.25 at lag 1, about 0.01 at lag 100) and strongest in the high-sigma_l
  cells.

**Choices (calibration).**
- L_max: each stock-day contributes lags l <= min(2000, N_sd / 10), and L_max is the last lag with at
  least 20 contributing stock-days (46 to 562 by cell). The cell-median reading of "N / 10" would stop at
  lags 1 to 155.
- Fit range [10, l_hi]: below lag 10 the fast part dominates. l_hi is L_max, cut at the last lag before
  C(l) first fails to exceed 2 bootstrap SE.
- Local Whittle on stock-days with N >= 500 (bandwidth m = N^0.65 >= 56), pooled over all of the cell's
  stock-days.
- log q demeaned per stock-day before its ACF, so stock-level size differences do not enter.
- Sign model fGn + AR(1) (+ white), least squares on the relative error of r = sin(pi C / 2), grids H in
  [0.51, 0.995], phi in [0.02, 0.98]. Size copula w rho_H on 25 log-spaced lags.

## 3. Generators (`tools/est/gen3.py`)

Every generator returns `Day(eps, q, lm, ok)`, the inputs of `moments.day_moments`. `panel(gen, cell,
n_stocks, n_days, seed)` draws each day's N from the cell's stock-day N values. Sizes are log-normal with
the cell's (mu, sigma_l), with options `size_kind="empirical"` (resampled from the cell's pooled sizes) and
`copula=True` (Gaussian copula with the cell's log q ACF).

- **G0**, monotone null: m_n = theta sum_{k<n} (1 + n - k)^-beta eps_k q_k^psi + noise random walk.
  Defaults psi = 0.5, beta = (1 - gamma_gen) / 2, theta = 1 / E[q^psi], noise sd 1 per run (all **Choice**).
- **GT**, tent oracle, a **Choice** (power device, not a mechanism): beta_b = max(0, beta_max - c |l_b -
  l_star|) on 12 equal log-size bins over [mu - 3 sigma_l, mu + 5 sigma_l], one FFT convolution per distinct
  beta, so c = 0 reproduces G0's arrays exactly. Ground-truth setting: beta_max 0.7, c 0.1, l_star = mu +
  sigma_l, psi 0.5. The argument is in `docs/phase3_oracle.md`.
- **GF**, informed flow (P-F world). What is implemented: each run is informed with probability 0.1,
  independently of everything. An informed run's sign is a fair coin independent of all other signs and
  sizes. Other runs carry sign(g), with g the cell's sign model refitted to C(l) / 0.9^2, so the mixture
  keeps the cell's C(l). Only informed runs move the mid, permanently, by eps q^psi / E[q^psi], plus the
  noise walk. Every pair of distinct runs then involves an independent coin, so K_a(T) = T x const exactly
  and zeta_K(a) = 1 for every a at every T.
- **GF_mb**, mbmodel's existing informed component (Paper I Sec. 6.2): PAPER2 metaorders with theta = 0,
  Gamma = 0, sigma_F = 1, rho = 0.5, a permanent dF per metaorder after its first child.
  **Measurement:** it is NOT a P-F world at these horizons (see section 4). The spec asked to use an
  existing mbmodel configuration if one exists. One exists, but its zeta_K is 1.08 to 1.18, so GF is the
  run-level construction above (**Choice**) and GF_mb is kept for the record.
- **GM**, Paper II model: `mbmodel.PAPER2` ("GM"), and beta_clip = (-0.6, 0.99) with Gamma = 0
  ("GM_floor_G0") and Gamma = 0.1 ("GM_floor_G01"). A day is the first N trades of an mbmodel day long
  enough to hold them.

## 4. Ground truth

Days of N = 10^6 runs; a in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3}; T = 2^0 .. 2^12 (**Choice**:
powers of 2, so the three OLS ranges [8, 64], [16, 256], [64, 1024] are 4 or 5 grid points each). Standard
errors come from a delete-one-day jackknife.

**Measurement: the specified SE target (< 0.005 for every local slope, every a, every T up to 4096) is out
of reach on this machine.** An 8-day pilot of G0 at Q-T3S1 gave SE 0.075 for the a = 0 slope 2048 -> 4096
(about 1800 days for 0.005) and up to 1.19 at a = 3. There q^(2a) under log-normal sizes with sigma_l about
1.5 is carried by a handful of runs per day. **Choice:** the stopping rule covers local slopes with a <= 1
and upper T <= 128, with a cap of 64 days (48 for the cell-free mbmodel runs). The SE of every slope is
saved, and the table says whether the rule was met.

**Choice:** GF_mb and the three GM variants use their own signs and sizes; the cell enters only through day
lengths, which the ground truth replaces by 10^6. So one run serves every cell, cell "(all)". Ground truth
uses i.i.d. log-normal sizes (no copula).

## 8. All Measurements and Choices in one place

Measurements (things that surprised me):
1. Thin stocks: the median N per stock-day is 18 to 1557 by cell.
2. Sign ACF: a fast part to lag about 10, then a near-flat tail that is mostly the variance of the
   stock-day mean sign. Power-law gamma -0.08 to 0.35 against local Whittle 0.70 to 0.94.
3. The sign model's fGn component sits at H = 0.995 in 8 of 18 cells.
4. mbmodel's existing informed component (GF_mb) is not a P-F world at finite T: zeta_K 1.05 to 1.18.
5. The specified SE target is out of reach (about 1800 days for one large-T slope at a = 0; hopeless at a = 3).
6. The first GT scan found no V because of my bin range; with bins to mu + 5 sigma_l there are V's.
7. The V minimum sits 0.5 to 0.65 later in a than the tilt heuristic predicts.
8. GT's V is significant in every cell on [8, 64] and [16, 256]; G0 is monotone everywhere.
9. GF is 1 within SE for a <= 1.5 but unmeasurable at a >= 2 with real sigma_l, even with 6.4 x 10^7 runs.
10. Paper II's model gives no V: PAPER2 falls in a, and the beta-floor variants rise in a.

Choices:
1. L_max via per-stock-day N / 10 with at least 20 stock-days; power-fit range [10, l_hi].
2. Local Whittle on stock-days with N >= 500; log q demeaned per stock-day.
3. Sign model fGn + AR(1) + white, H <= 0.995; size copula w rho_H.
4. G0: psi = 0.5, beta = (1 - gamma_gen) / 2, theta = 1 / E[q^psi], noise sd 1 per run.
5. GT: 12 bins over [mu - 3 sigma_l, mu + 5 sigma_l], beta floored at 0. Truth setting beta_max 0.7, c 0.1,
   l_star = mu + sigma_l, psi 0.5. GT itself is a power device, not a mechanism.
6. GF: run-level informed share 0.1, informed signs independent coins, permanent impact.
7. Truth: T = powers of 2 to 4096, i.i.d. log-normal sizes. Stopping-rule domain a <= 1, T <= 128. Caps
   64 days (48 cell-free). GF_mb and GM computed once (cell-free).
8. Collapse: T = 16..1024, first 4 days, chi grid 0.25..2.5 step 0.005. a*(T) refined by a parabola
   through the grid maximum and its neighbours.
9. V criterion: depth < -2 jackknife SE. Scan: 16 days per setting.
